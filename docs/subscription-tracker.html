03 · Money

Every subscription and recurring bill, in one place.

Subscription Tracker is a free Android app for keeping track of what renews, when, and what it really costs. Add subscriptions and bills by hand, see a monthly and yearly total you can compare, and get a reminder before each renewal.

In one line: Subscription Tracker is a free, offline Android app that records your recurring bills, converts them into comparable monthly and yearly totals, and reminds you before a renewal — without connecting to your bank.

Download the APK Source on GitHub

Version 1.1.0, free, Android 8.0 and later. Distributed as a signed APK from the project’s GitHub releases.

What it does

  • One list of everything that recurs — streaming, cloud storage, insurance, the Wi-Fi bill, anything with a renewal date.
  • A monthly and yearly total that you can actually compare, because each entry is normalised from its own billing cycle.
  • Upcoming renewals in date order, so nothing arrives as a surprise.
  • Optional reminders before a payment is due, scheduled on the phone.
  • Works offline, with no bank connection and no account.

Why the totals are normalised

“₹149 a month” and “₹1,499 a year” are not comparable until they are on the same timeline, and a list of mixed cycles makes it very easy to underestimate what you spend. Every entry therefore keeps its real billing cycle and is also converted to a monthly equivalent, so a yearly plan, a weekly plan and a monthly plan all contribute to one honest figure. The yearly total is that same figure across twelve months.

The result is a number that answers the question people actually have: what do all of these cost me, together, this month?

Manual by design

There is no bank connection, no SMS scraping and no account aggregation. You add each subscription yourself, which sounds like more work and is in fact the privacy feature: nothing about your finances is ever uploaded, and no third party is told which services you pay for. The app does not request an account from you, and its data lives in its own storage on the phone.

That also means the numbers are only as good as the list. The app deliberately does not guess at subscriptions from your messages or notifications.

Common questions

Does Subscription Tracker connect to my bank?

No. You add each subscription and its renewal date yourself. The app has no bank integration, no SMS access and no account aggregation, so your financial accounts are never linked to it.

How are monthly and yearly totals calculated?

Each entry keeps its own billing cycle and is converted to a monthly equivalent, so weekly, monthly and yearly plans can be compared and added together. The yearly total is that monthly figure across twelve months.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is built to work without a connection; subscriptions and totals are stored on the device.

Will it detect subscriptions automatically?

No. Entries are added by hand, on purpose — automatic detection would mean reading your messages or connecting to your bank, and the app does neither.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download and use, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.
