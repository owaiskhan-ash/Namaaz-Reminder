A calm, offline vaccine log for little ones.

Vaccine Routine keeps a child’s vaccination record on your phone, helps you plan the next dose and reminds you when it is due. India’s National Immunization Schedule is offered as a suggestion — never a prescription.

In one line: Vaccine Routine is a free, offline-first vaccine log and reminder for babies and children, with India’s National Immunization Schedule available as optional suggestions rather than medical advice.

Download the app Source on GitHub

Free. Available for Android and on the web. Distributed from the project’s GitHub releases.

What it does

  • A local vaccine record for a child, kept on the phone rather than in an account.
  • A plan of upcoming doses, so the next visit is not a surprise.
  • Gentle reminders on the dates you set.
  • India’s National Immunization Schedule as optional suggestions you can accept, ignore or edit.
  • Offline-first: the record opens and saves with no connection.

The schedule is a suggestion, not a prescription

The app can show the doses in India’s National Immunization Schedule as a starting point, because an empty form is not a helpful place to begin. It is a template, not an instruction. Timing varies with the child, the state and the vaccines available, and some children follow a different schedule for medical reasons.

Nothing in the app is medical advice, and it does not replace the health worker who sees your child. Treat every date it suggests as something to confirm with them, and record what actually happened rather than what was planned.

Private by construction

A child’s health record is about the most sensitive list a phone can hold, which is why Vaccine Routine is built without an account, a sync service or an analytics SDK. Records are written to the app’s own storage on the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled. There is nothing to log in to, so there is no server holding a copy.

That trade-off is deliberate: no cloud sync means no account to leak and nothing to subscribe to. If you change phones, the record moves with Android’s own backup or not at all — the app does not upload it anywhere.

Common questions

Is the vaccine schedule in the app medical advice?

No. India’s National Immunization Schedule is included as an optional template, not as an instruction. Timing differs between children, states and vaccines, so confirm every date with the health worker who sees your child.

Where is my child’s record stored?

On your device, in the app’s own private storage. There is no account and no sync service, so no copy is uploaded to a server, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Vaccine Routine is offline-first: the record opens and saves with no network, and reminders are scheduled on the device.

Can I edit the suggested schedule?

Yes. The national schedule is a starting point you can accept, ignore or edit, so the record reflects what actually happened rather than what was planned.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download and use, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.
